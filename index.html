Ananta Katha

The endless story: animated tales from India and beyond.

Ananta Katha is a YouTube channel that retells myths, legends, lost kingdoms and unsolved mysteries, mostly from India's history and traditions, as narrated, illustrated stories.

This site also hosts the information for Ananta Katha Pipeline, a private tool the channel owner uses to prepare, upload and schedule videos on the Ananta Katha channel and to read that channel's analytics. The tool is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Ananta Katha Pipeline · Last updated 18 November 2026

What this app is

Ananta Katha Pipeline is a private tool operated by the owner of the Ananta Katha YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner to manage that channel. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

Google data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • YouTube (upload and read-only): to upload, schedule and set thumbnails and captions for videos on the owner's own channel, and to list those videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only): to read view, watch-time and subscriber statistics for the owner's own channel.
  • Google Sheets: to read and write the owner's content-planning spreadsheet.
  • Google Drive (files created by the app only): to save finished video files into a folder in the owner's Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used solely to produce and publish videos on the Ananta Katha channel and to show the owner how those videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only by the app's automated jobs.

Ananta Katha Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics figures are kept in the owner's own Google Sheet for as long as the owner chooses. Temporary video files are deleted when each automated job finishes. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer access any Google data.

Viewers of the channel

The app does not collect personal information about people who watch Ananta Katha videos. YouTube reports only aggregated statistics to the channel owner.
